Focus:
- Judge whether the leading problem is truly cardiac rather than a non-cardiac mimic.
- Give extra weight to ECG, rhythm findings, troponin, hemodynamics, exertional pattern, and structural clues.
- Distinguish ischemic, rhythm-related, and structural cardiac processes from broader chest or dizziness complaints.
- Do not overcall a precise cardiac disease if the objective evidence is thin.
- If certainty is limited, return the most specific supported cardiac syndrome.
